Keep researching with your own wallet

The sponsored allowance has ended. You can call Parallel directly through MPP. Download your question, then run this command with a funded Tempo wallet.

tempo request https://parallelmpp.dev/api/responses \
  --request POST --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data @parallel-request.json --max-spend 0.01 \
  --network mainnet --payment-intent charge \
  --payment-token USDC.e

Under the hood

Three useful primitives.
One complete request.

Identity

World ID

Authenticates the account and unlocks its allowance. Verification uses the World IdP and MPP’s World ID attestation extension.

Research

Parallel

Returns an answer grounded in live web sources. This prototype uses the production Responses endpoint at its low reasoning tier.

Payment

Tempo + MPP

Settles the sponsored service charge per request. A successful response includes a payment receipt you can inspect.
